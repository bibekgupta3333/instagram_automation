# Brief and Angle Protocol

> **Studio adaptation.** `CREATOR-PROVIDED` evidence does not exist for a faceless brand;
> use `VERIFIED` (a supplied source with a date) or `OPEN`. With topic-only input every
> quantitative claim is `OPEN` and is removed from the JSON, not marked: the pipeline speaks
> every field aloud or on screen, so `[CREATOR: confirm ...]` markers must never reach the
> Script JSON. The differentiation thesis must name the original element (mechanism order,
> our own experiment or data, a narrower viewer situation) that keeps the video clear of
> YouTube's inauthentic-content policy. The channel's niche bounds every angle.

## One-video constraint

A video must have one dominant transformation:

```text
Viewer arrives believing/doing/feeling A.
Evidence and explanation move the viewer toward B.
The viewer leaves able to repeat or do C.
```

If C contains “and” joining unrelated outcomes, narrow the video.

## Brief schema

```text
TOPIC:
FORMAT / TARGET DURATION:
EXACT VIEWER SITUATION:
CURRENT BELIEF OR FAILURE MODE:
CLICK TENSION:
PACKAGING PROMISE:
ONE TAKEAWAY:
PROOF MECHANISM:
PRIMARY CTA:
VOICE CONFIDENCE:
EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE:
```

## Angle selection

Evaluate candidate angles on five dimensions:

- Existing viewer demand
- Specificity of promised outcome
- Strength of available proof
- Distinction from common coverage
- Fit with creator authority and voice

Do not select an angle merely because it sounds contrarian. A contradiction without evidence is decoration.

## Competitive differentiation thesis

Write one internal sentence:

```text
This video improves on common coverage by [specific difference] using [proof or mechanism].
```

Possible differences include a newer source base, a clearer mechanism, a real demonstration, a counterexample, a narrower viewer situation, or an honest constraint omitted elsewhere.

## Evidence ledger

```text
ID | CLAIM | STATUS | SOURCE / OWNER | DATE | VISUAL PROOF | SCRIPT LOCATION
```

Allowed statuses: `VERIFIED`, `CREATOR-PROVIDED`, `OPINION`, `OPEN`.

For `OPEN`, retain `[CREATOR: confirm ...]` in the draft or remove the claim.
