### Palette, setting and tone

- **Palette:** pond teal `#5EC8C0`, buttercup yellow `#FFD25E` and coral `#FF8C7A`, on cream and warm-white backgrounds.
- **Setting:** the family's round-doored otter cottage beside a calm pond (hallway, bathroom with pale teal tiles and a round window, kitchen, bedroom), its garden, and a pond-side park.
- **Tone:** warm, cosy and playful. The camera moves slowly, there are no stings or jump zooms, and the lighting stays soft with no flashing. Every episode shows a positive routine with a grown-up in frame for water, food and stairs. No commercial pressure and no villains.
- **Shared style suffix** (appended to every prompt): *Soft rounded 3D clay and plush animation style, big expressive eyes, bright warm soft lighting, pastel teal, buttercup yellow and coral palette, slow gentle camera movement, calm toddler-friendly mood, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.*

### Lyrics

> **Intro (call and response)**
> Is it bath time? — *Yes, it's bath time!*
>
> **Verse 1**
> Mum fills the tub up, drip, drip, drop,
> Mum checks it's warm, then sit down, plop!
>
> **Chorus**
> Bubbles on my tummy, bubbles on my nose,
> Pop! Pop! Pop! And away it goes!
>
> **Verse 2 (call and response, counting)**
> Who's got bubbles? — *Me, me, me!*
> Count with Fern: one, two, three!
>
> **Chorus**
> Bubbles on my tummy, bubbles on my nose,
> Pop! Pop! Pop! And away it goes!
>
> **Verse 3**
> Wash my paws and wash my ears,
> Cosy towel, big cuddle!
>
> **Tag**
> Bye-bye, bubbles. Pop! ... pop!

The song models routines toddlers can copy: a grown-up fills the bath and checks the temperature with an elbow, the child sits rather than jumps or stands, the grown-up keeps foam away from the eyes, and the grown-up lifts the child out.

## 4. Rights, safety and pipeline notes

- **Originality:** the lyrics, melody, characters, names and setting are new. The title and hook avoid "Splish Splash" (Bobby Darin, 1958) and "Bubble Pop". Any backing track must be original or properly licensed, not a modern arrangement of another song.
- **Imitable safety:** Mum is in frame in every water shot except the cutaways that show no characters. Pebble always sits in shallow water. Foam stays away from the eyes. There is no climbing, jumping or standing in the tub.
- **Publishing blocker:** `assert_publishable` requires `made_for_kids=False`, so this channel cannot be published through the current gate. That is by design. A kids publishing path needs a deliberate, human-reviewed change. Do not relax the gate to make room for it.
- **Voice:** the pack uses `af_river`, because `studio/stages/tts.check_voice` accepts only Kokoro US voices. The intended UK British voices (`bf_*`) need a reviewed change to that allow-list. Sung vocals come from the separate music tool.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_brand_packs.py::test_all_ten_packs_exist_and_validate` expects exactly the ten adult slugs, so it fails while this pack exists. The youtube-script skill's `references/channel-rules.md` must also be regenerated (`.claude/skills/youtube-script/scripts/build_channel_rules.py`). The Orchestrator decides both.
