This is a design basis, not evidence that this proposed show improves learning outcomes. [Head Start framework](https://headstart.gov/school-readiness/article/science-preschool).

## Characters and storytelling

- **Tavi the mole** notices details and asks patient questions.
- **Pella the moth** makes a guess and is comfortable changing her mind.
- **Bram the beetle** builds a simple test and revises it when something fails.

The characters are fictional, with no claims of human credentials. Create original silhouettes, palettes, voices and artwork; do not imitate JJ, CoComelon's branding, recognisable songs or another creator's likeness. Reuse approved character art for continuity, while giving each episode a distinct story and demonstration.

An eventual 3–4-minute episode introduces a concrete puzzle, invites a prediction, demonstrates a test, explains the result and offers an optional caregiver-supported activity. Leave time to respond. The initial 45–60-second prototype tests the visual and narration approach, not the complete episode format.

## Six starter episodes

| Episode | Learning objective | Distinct story action |
|---|---|---|
| The Shadow That Missed Picnic | Light and shadows | Move a cardboard flower between a light and a picnic mat |
| Pella's Mixed-Up Leaf Mail | Classifying objects | Sort fallen leaves by shape, then by texture |
| The Ramp to Bram's Door | Compare surfaces | Compare one large wooden wheel on smooth and fabric-covered ramps |
| Who Soaked the Rain Blanket? | Absorption | Compare cloth and waxed paper with equal spoonfuls of water |
| The Bridge for One More Friend | Testing and revision | Compare flat and folded paper bridges using large wooden blocks |
| The Quietest Meadow Parade | Materials and sound | Compare gentle sounds from cardboard, fabric and wooden props |

Verify the physical demonstrations before illustrating results. Adapt materials for age and supervision; no small loose parts, tasting, mains electricity or unsupervised water activities. Avoid flashing effects, frightening hooks, humiliation, purchase pressure and promises to diagnose or treat developmental conditions.

## First prototype brief

Use **Pella's Mixed-Up Leaf Mail**: four to six approved scenes, a single narrator and gentle camera moves. Pella cannot find matching envelopes; Tavi sorts leaves by shape; Bram notices texture gives another useful grouping. The conclusion is that the same collection can be sorted in different ways.

Write the narration before image prompts. Record scene assets and their provenance; manually reject visual drift and malformed objects. Put any labels in overlays, never AI-generated image text. The existing still-image pipeline can illustrate this story, but is not an articulated character-animation or singing system.
