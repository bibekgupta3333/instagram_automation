# Megabuild Blueprint — 20 script treatments

Status: planning / unverified before filming. Original English treatments for US adult audiences; these are development outlines, not complete word-for-word narrations. Each standard episode targets 7–12 minutes. Structures: visual mystery, reverse engineering, comparison, constraint ladder, and prediction. Their use adapts premise clarity and earned answers without imitating another creator's voice or scenes.

Production convention: all diagrams, animations, maps, schedules, screens, and thumbnail compositions below are proposed original, manually authored assets, labelled **illustrative; not to scale**. They are not engineering drawings or claims of existing renderer capability. Any hypothetical values must be labelled assumptions; no research experiments have been executed. No construction procedures, operating settings, restricted-site access, aviation, or household-system instruction. Film only from permitted public locations or with written permission. No copied engineering drawings, television clips, borrowed drone footage, logos, or recognizable real faces. Any AI background prompt includes “no text”; titles and labels are added in editing, never generated inside images. No thumbnails imply measured results the film cannot substantiate. AI disclosure and human review precede any future publication; no guaranteed performance or copyright safety.
