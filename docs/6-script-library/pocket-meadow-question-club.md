## K01-01 — Pella’s Mixed-Up Leaf Mail

**Format & length:** 3:20 standard 16:9 story; 40 s self-contained vertical discovery cut. First production milestone remains one 45–60 s local prototype, not all twenty episodes.

**Evergreen angle:** Sorting and descriptive language; the everyday question remains useful. Recheck reference observations, safe materials and educator feedback before production.

**Title & thumbnail A/B:** Title: “Pella’s Mixed-Up Leaf Mail”. A: leaf beside two differently shaped envelopes; B: one leaf with two sorting paths. Original paper-textured meadow art, calm framing, one focal subject per option, no text in generated images; optional overlay “Look Closely”.

**Hook (0:00–0:10):** Narrator: “These leaves belong in different envelopes. Can we sort them in more than one way?” Opening shot: the central object appears in the meadow immediately.

**Attention magnet:** Can one leaf belong to a shape group and a texture group? Give a partial observation before the midpoint; resolve it at 2:40–3:20 rather than withholding all value.

**Story beats:**

- **0:00–0:30:** Tracking shot: Pella’s cart stops at an envelope that does not match. Narrator: “The mail is mixed up, but nothing is lost.”

- **0:30–1:10:** Overhead: Tavi groups broad and narrow paper leaves. Pause for a spoken or pointed prediction before revealing each match.

- **1:10–2:00:** Macro: Bram notices smooth and ridged textures across both shape piles. Narrator: “Our first rule is useful. Could another rule work too?”

- **2:00–2:40:** Switch to a fresh tabletop; sort the same objects by texture without calling either group better or worse.

- **2:40–3:20:** Return to the original leaf: trace its place under both rules. Show that classification depends on the chosen attribute.


**Payoff & final line:** Resolve the opening question through the final demonstrated comparison above. Narrator: “We changed the sorting rule, not the leaf.” No subscription demand or forced cliffhanger.

**Short cut:** Show one leaf, compare two rules, then reveal both valid groupings. Finish within 40 s; the answer belongs in this cut too.

**Evidence & rights:** Verify classifications are visually consistent; create all leaf/envelope artwork from scratch. Review developmental fit against the register and with an educator. All scenes are original fiction/illustration; use approved character assets, no borrowed songs or character designs.
