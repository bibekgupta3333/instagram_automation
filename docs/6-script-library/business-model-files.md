# Business Model Files — 20 Script Treatments

Status: planning / unverified before filming. These are detailed treatments, not complete narration scripts. All businesses, transactions, amounts, customer behavior and experiments below are fictional teaching scenarios unless a later production draft supplies a dated primary citation. No experiment has been executed. Real-company figures require public filings, reporting periods, page references and independent arithmetic checks; never substitute these examples for company results. These explain business mechanisms, without investment, business-purchase or income advice.

Production conventions: faceless, adult US audience; manila case files, black ink and ledger-green diagrams. All diagrams, dashboards, interfaces and animation are proposed manual authoring, requiring production feasibility review; this document makes no renderer-capability claim. Create original tabletop footage and graphics; obtain written permission for any outside premises or identifiable participants. No company footage, logos, famous faces, copied layouts, borrowed music or imitation of another creator. Thumbnails use one focal subject, no numerical performance claims, and at most three manually composited words; any generated background must contain no text. Keep AI disclosure on and Not made for kids. No affiliate promotion is planned. Maintain an asset/permission ledger; original construction reduces some risks but cannot guarantee copyright safety.

## C03-01 — What Does a Warehouse Membership Actually Sell?
**Format & length:** 16:9, 10 minutes; 40-second vertical.
**Evergreen angle:** Why pay before shopping? Update fees and filing periods.
**Title & thumbnail A/B:** A “What Membership Buys”: overhead blank membership card, “ENTRY.” B “The Renewal Question”: edge-lit card standing upright, “AGAIN?”
**Hook (0:00–0:10):** “This store makes a sale before anyone touches a shelf.” Push toward a membership card.
**Attention magnet:** What must renewals support? Resolve the ledger at 8:00.
**Story beats:** 0:00–2:00, reverse engineering: separate membership and merchandise receipts in a desk diagram. 2:00–4:00, construct an annual cohort; VO: “The first payment opens the door.” 4:00–6:00, remove departing members while keeping staffing obligations visible; overhead cards expose the complication. 6:00–8:00, acknowledge merchandise contribution and service value; VO: “A fee is revenue, not a free-standing profit machine.” 8:00–10:00, rebuild the combined ledger with every assumption labeled, revealing renewal dependence without attributing all profit to fees.
**Payoff & final line:** Membership and shopping economics interact. “The card sells access; renewal asks whether that access delivered.”
**Short cut:** Ask why entry costs money; show both revenue trays and shared costs; answer with the renewal tradeoff.
**Evidence & rights:** S1/S5; verify membership recognition and renewal definitions in a selected warehouse filing. Original card, shelves and ledger; hypothetical cohort only.
