Start with [22 — Directing and rights playbook](22-directing-and-rights-playbook.md). Each episode includes recommended runtime, evergreen relevance and refresh triggers, title and thumbnail A/B, an exact spoken opening, an attention magnet with a resolution point, five timed story beats, selected narration, an exact closing line, a complete short-form adaptation and evidence/asset checks.

These are **detailed script treatments**, not fully timed word-for-word narration, finished research, generated thumbnails or publishable videos. All experiments are proposed unless a later production record establishes otherwise. Convert a selected treatment to a verified narration and shot list before a real render. The source registers identify research starting points; a source URL alone does not verify every proposed statement.

## What to adapt from high-retention creators

Use a concrete promise, early visual confirmation, a meaningful constraint, new evidence as the story progresses and a satisfying answer. All writing and thumbnail compositions here are newly drafted; no copied scripts, clips, songs, famous faces or channel branding are needed. The shared guide explains the research and its limits. For preschool work, use calm curiosity and room to respond rather than urgency or reward pressure.

## Rights and release status

No one can guarantee zero copyright claims or strikes. Every plan calls for original or appropriately licensed assets and episode-specific verification. Keep a rights ledger; facts researched on a website do not grant permission to copy that site's images, charts or music. A/B thumbnail briefs describe alternatives to create, not completed tests. Made-for-kids content is ineligible for YouTube's native A/B testing; review those alternatives with adult caregivers.

Nothing in this library changes brand packs, loads a model, approves a job or publishes content. The kids proposal needs correctly implemented made-for-kids support before release. Follow the [production requirements](../4-production/21-kids-pilot-production.md) and existing human approval rules.
