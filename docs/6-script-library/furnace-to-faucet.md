# Furnace to Faucet — 20 evergreen treatments

Original editorial treatments, not complete word-for-word narration. Each standard episode targets 6–10 minutes and has an independently understandable vertical cut. Five story structures rotate: reverse mystery, controlled comparison, object journey, prediction/reveal, and constraint ladder. Clear comparisons, immediate visual explanations, escalating constraints, and earned answers are broad retention principles; no creator's voice, scenes, or packaging is copied. Timing is a directing target pending a full script and timed read.

All schematics, timelines, cutaways, thumbnails, and moving graphics below are planned manual authoring tasks, not claims of current renderer support. Use walnut structure, copper plumbing, blue HVAC, and one highlighted household system. Label hypothetical scenarios and illustrative arrows; they are not measured flows or completed experiments. No repairs, equipment opening, settings recommendations, gas/combustion procedures, live electrical work, bypasses, or diagnosis. No credentials, savings guarantees, fabricated results, or urgency. Stay inside the home's systems, rather than civic infrastructure or aviation.

Thumbnail A/B concepts each have one focal object and 0–3 overlay words. No logos or famous faces. AI art, if later commissioned, must say “no text”; typography is added separately. All assets described as owned are to be created, with authorship/project files retained. No manufacturer tracings or agency artwork reuse. Production requires an asset manifest and claim ledger, AI disclosure, and Not made for kids metadata. These plans authorize no approval or publishing.
